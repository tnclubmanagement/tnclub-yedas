    <h2>Little things worth sharing.</h2>
    <p>Yedas began with a simple habit: when something catches my interest,
    I write it down. A great angle for a phone photo, a memorable badminton
    match, a tucked-away place to eat discovered on a trip — each becomes
    a short, practical story with something you can try for yourself.</p>
    <p>This is not a professional review site or a long personal diary.
    Yedas is simply a collection of small things worth remembering.</p>
